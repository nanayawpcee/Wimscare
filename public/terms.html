Terms & Data Policy

How WIMScare handles your account and your data.

Version 2026-07-26 · applies to every member, staff and administrator account on the platform

1. What this covers

This page explains how WIMScare — the Welfare Information Management System — handles the account and personal information of anyone who signs in: members, and the Supervisors, Accountants and Administrators who run a welfare organization's console. By creating or activating an account you agree to the practices described here.

2. Who's responsible for your data

Your welfare organization (a hospital, church, association or similar body) decides what information to collect about its members and staff, and is responsible for keeping it accurate. REKONSTECH Trading and Consultancy Services operates the WIMScare platform your organization's administrators use to do that. Your organization's administrator is your first point of contact for questions or requests about your data.

3. Information we collect

  • Identity — full name, email address, phone number.
  • Membership — member number, department, role, join date.
  • Financial — contribution history, payment method and reference, receipt numbers.
  • Claims — benefit applications, any supporting documents you upload, approval history and payout records.
  • Profile details you choose to add — bank details for payouts, an emergency contact, family or beneficiary information.
  • Account security — a hashed password, phone verification status, sign-in history, and an audit trail of changes made to your account.

We don't collect anything beyond what your organization's administrators enter or that you submit yourself through your profile, claims and preferences.

4. How your information is used

  • To track your contributions, apply any benefit limits, and move your claims through your organization's approval chain.
  • To send account notifications — activation links, claim status updates, contribution receipts, and phone verification codes — according to your notification preferences.
  • To produce reports for your organization's administrators, both aggregate and per-member.
  • To maintain an audit trail of sensitive actions, for accountability.

5. Who can see your data

  • Staff of your own organization can see the records their role and permissions allow — an Administrator sees more than a Supervisor, who sees more than an Accountant handling only contributions, and so on.
  • The WIMScare platform team can access organization data only for support, billing and platform maintenance — every such access is written to the immutable audit trail.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing, and your organization's data is never visible to any other organization on the platform.

6. Data retention — including after account deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and afterward as described here:

  • You can request that your account be deleted at any time. Your organization's administrator reviews member/staff requests; an administrator's own request (or a request to delete the whole organization) is reviewed by the WIMScare team.
  • Approving a deletion request locks the account immediately — nobody can sign in with it again.

Approving a deletion does not erase your contribution, claim or ledger history. Those records stay in your organization's books, correctly attributed to you, so that financial totals, audit trails and compliance reports stay accurate, any benefit you already received remains properly documented, and other members' shared totals are never thrown off by records disappearing.

  • Uploaded claim documents, contribution receipts and audit-log entries are kept indefinitely unless your organization's administrator removes a specific file, or the organization itself is deleted.
  • Your organization's administrators can trigger backups, and the platform also runs them automatically, capturing organization data (including uploaded files) for disaster recovery.
  • If an entire organization is deleted, all of its data is kept in the same way — locked away from everyone except the WIMScare platform team — for the same audit and continuity reasons.

7. Your rights

  • View your own profile, contribution history and claim status at any time.
  • Correct your personal details yourself, or ask your administrator to.
  • Request deletion of your account — see Section 6 for exactly what that does.
  • Verify your phone number and optionally require an SMS code at every sign-in, for extra security.
  • Choose which email notifications you receive, from your Preferences.

8. Security

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text; activation and password-reset links are single-use, hashed, and expire quickly.
  • Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies that page scripts cannot read.
  • Optional two-factor authentication (an SMS code at sign-in) is available to every account.
  • Sensitive actions — role changes, permission grants, backups, deletions — are written to a tenant-scoped audit log your administrators can review.

No system is perfectly secure and we can't guarantee against every possible incident, but the platform is built to limit what any single compromised credential can do.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform changes. When we do, the version date at the top changes, and you'll be asked to review and accept it again the next time you sign in.

10. A note on legal review

This policy describes, in plain language, how the WIMScare platform actually stores and handles data today. It is not a substitute for formal legal advice. If your organization operates under a specific data-protection law, you should have this policy reviewed by your own legal counsel before relying on it for compliance purposes.
