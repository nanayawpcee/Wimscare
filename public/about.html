About WIMScare

Built so no member's contribution goes unseen.

WIMScare — the Welfare Information Management System — replaces spreadsheets and paper ledgers with one trusted platform for contributions, claims and reporting. Made for hospitals, churches and associations that run welfare programs for their people.

For members

Your welfare, in the open

See every cedi you've contributed, apply for wedding or funeral support in minutes, and follow each claim from submission to payout — with notifications along the way.

For administrators

Full oversight, less paperwork

Manage members and roles, record contributions individually or in bulk, process claims with a clear review workflow, and keep the accounts ledger tidy.

For the board

Reports you can stand behind

Every action is logged in an audit trail. Generate contribution, claims and financial reports, and export them to CSV, Excel or PDF for meetings and audits.

What the platform covers

Contribution tracking

Individual and bulk CSV entry, scheduled dues, receipts and full member history.

Claims processing

Applications with document uploads, staged review workflow and status notifications.

Reporting & analytics

Filterable reports with CSV, Excel and PDF export, plus visual summaries.

Member management

Invitations, activation, profiles with family and beneficiary records.

Financial accounting

Income and expense ledger, financial statements and contribution summaries.

Role-based access

Admin, supervisor, accountant and member roles with granular permissions.

Security & privacy

✓Role-based access control across every screen
✓Secure cookie-based authentication
✓Single-use, time-limited activation links
✓Encrypted password storage
✓Audit trails for all administrative actions
